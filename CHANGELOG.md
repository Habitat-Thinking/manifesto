## 1.1 (September 2026)

- **Changed meaning:** "Working from here" no longer frames the assessment as reaching a higher rung. It asks whether a team's habitat can safely carry the work it has, and whether each piece of work is being done in a way that fits it.
- **Changed meaning:** Added the principle "Let the work choose how it is done".
- **Changed meaning:** Added the principle "Anyone can steer the work; answering for it takes understanding", and widened "What this is" so that thinking well applies to people who steer the system as well as those who build it.
- **Clarified:** "Understanding is a deliverable" now says that not every change needs to be understood in the same way, but every system needs people who still understand it.
- **Reworded:** The paragraph on scale describes habitats that adjoin, overlap and share a climate, replacing the image of rooms inside buildings. Its two claims are unchanged.
